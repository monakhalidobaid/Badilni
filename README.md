# Badilni (بادلني)

An innovative mobile platform designed to breathe new life into used books. **Badilni** allows readers to exchange physical books or borrow digital ones, creating a sustainable community for knowledge sharing. Built with Flutter and Firebase, it features a unique automated lending system for E-books.

### Features
* Physical Book Exchange:** List your pre-loved books and find others to swap with.
* Smart E-Lending:** Borrow digital books with a custom "auto-return" logic that expires access after 30 days.
* User-Centric Profiles:** Track your reading history, manage your "library," and chat with other book lovers.

---

### The Process
As a team of students, we noticed a common problem: books are expensive, and once read, they often just sit on shelves gathering dust. We wanted to create something that felt more like a "community library" than a marketplace. 

I led a team of 4 developers through the entire lifecycle—from initial wireframes to the final APK. The biggest challenge? Implementing the **E-book lending logic**. We didn't want a simple download; we wanted a system that felt like a real library. I designed the core logic where the app automatically revokes access to a digital file after the 30-day lending period, ensuring fair use.
